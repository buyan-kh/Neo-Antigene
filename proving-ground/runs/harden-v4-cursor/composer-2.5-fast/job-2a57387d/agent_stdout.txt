## Verdict

The reported list **cannot be trusted**. Benjamini–Hochberg is implemented correctly in `stats.py`, but **`analysis/prepare.py` drops every feature with raw two-sided p ≥ 0.05** before `run.py` ever sees them. FDR is then run on only those 34 survivors (`m = 34`), not on all **400** measured features. That inflates significance: the pipeline calls **34** hits; with BH on the full panel at 5% FDR there are **8**.
