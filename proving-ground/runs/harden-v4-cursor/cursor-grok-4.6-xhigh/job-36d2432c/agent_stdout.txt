Normalization covered serological aliases (`A3` → `HLA-A*03:01`, `Cw7` → `HLA-C*07:01`), compact codes (`A*0104`, `B0801`), missing `HLA-` / `*`, extra fields, case, and trailing spaces. `HLA-A*01:04N` and `HLA-A*02:01G` stayed distinct from the unsuffixed alleles. Payloads are attached to the original source.
