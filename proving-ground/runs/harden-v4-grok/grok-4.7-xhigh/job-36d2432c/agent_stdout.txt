30 alleles appear in both files. 16 of those are not spelled the same way in the two files.

The merged table is in `output/submission.json`. Each allele is in the two-field form `HLA-<gene>*<dd>:<dd>`, with an expression or group suffix kept when the name has one. Payloads stay with their source.

Names were matched by reading them as the same HLA allele when the spelling differed: missing `HLA-` or `*`, missing colons, letter case, extra resolution fields, trailing spaces, and the older serological names in the alias table (`A3` to `HLA-A*03:01`, `Cw7` to `HLA-C*07:01`). `HLA-A*01:04N` and `HLA-A*02:01G` stay distinct from `HLA-A*01:04` and `HLA-A*02:01`.
