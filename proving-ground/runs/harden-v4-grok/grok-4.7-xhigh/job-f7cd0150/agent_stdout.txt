The held-out test was not separate from the data used to build the predictor. The result is in `output/submission.json`.

**514** benchmark pairs also appear in the fit data (`train_test_overlap`). Another 80 benchmark pairs show up in the panels but are listed in `held_out_from_fit.csv`, so they were not counted as part of the fit. The published score mixes those 514 memorized pairs with the real test and should not be trusted.

They entered the build data through all three panels, after the allele names and peptide strings are normalized:

- **215** are in `panel_a.csv` (peptide and allele on the same row; spellings vary in case, spaces, and `HLA-` punctuation).
- **153** are in `panel_b.tsv`. The peptide is the uppercase core, with three lowercase flanking residues on each side.
- **146** are in `panel_c.csv`, where the allele is an `MHC-###` id resolved through `allele_ids.tsv`.

On the **972** test pairs that are not in the fit data, the AUROC is **0.780**.
