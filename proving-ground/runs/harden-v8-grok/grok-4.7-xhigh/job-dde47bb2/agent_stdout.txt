The reported neo-ORFs are wrong for every variant. The start of each new stretch is one codon too far downstream, because translation begins on the codon after the variant instead of on the codon that contains it.

The annotation and the cached coding-sequence spans agree, and the indels land on the right bases in the coding sequence. The error is in `downstream_peptide`: `zero_based // 3 + 1` both drops the variant codon and records that later index. The corrected stretch is the translation from the zero-based codon `(CDSPOS - 1) // 3` through the first stop. For `ENST00000000003:131` that codon is `TGA`, so the stretch is `*`.

Corrected results are in `output/submission.json`.
