## Verdict

**The reported list should not be trusted.** The pipeline applies Benjamini–Hochberg only to features that already passed an **uncorrected** p &lt; 0.05 gate, not to the full panel of 480 features.

### What goes wrong

`README.md` says FDR is controlled at 5% with BH over the screen. In practice:

1. **`bin/select_candidates.py`** keeps only features with raw `two_sided_p(...) < ALPHA` (0.05) — 41 of 480.
2. **`analysis/stats.py`** runs BH on that subset only. With m = 41, every one of those 41 gets q ≤ 0.05, so all 41 appear in `results/reported_significant.csv`.

Features never forwarded to `data/features.csv` are excluded from the multiplicity correction entirely, which inflates the hit list.

### Correct list (BH on all 480 features, q ≤ 0.05)

`feat_0103`, `feat_0123`, `feat_0129`, `feat_0398`, `feat_0405`, `feat_0433`, `feat_0451`, `feat_0472`

Written to [`output/submission.json`](output/submission.json) with `bug_class`: `correction_applied_after_prefiltering` and location `bin/select_candidates.py` lines 34–38 (the uncorrected prefilter). The z-test in `stats.py` matches the README; the flaw is the restricted hypothesis set before FDR.
