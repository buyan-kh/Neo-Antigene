## Verdict

**Drift detected.** All 12 variants in `reported_neoorfs.csv` are wrong.

## Mechanism

In `enumerate_downstream.py`, `downstream_peptide` sets `first_codon = zero_based // 3 + 1` and slices from `first_codon * 3`. That skips the codon that contains the variant and starts one codon **downstream**. The spec requires the neoORF to begin at the **first codon containing the variant** (zero-based codon index `zero_based // 3`).

The README’s note about stale `cds_offsets.py` is a red herring: cached CDS spans match `annotation.gff3` when transcript coordinates use `genomic − transcript_origin` (1000000).

## Magnitude

- **1 codon** downstream (both `start_offset` and peptide sequence are shifted by one codon).
