### Normalization (summary)
- **`gene_aliases.tsv`**: shorthand gene symbols (`A3`, `B7`, `Cw7`, …) → full canonical alleles from the TSV.
- **Legacy / compact forms**: `B1501`, `A3303`, `DRB10401`, `C*0702`, `A*2601`, etc. → two-field names.
- **Missing `*` / `HLA-`**: `HLA-A02:01`, `HLA-DRB115:01`, `HLA-B14:02`, …
- **`Cw` → `C`**: e.g. `HLA-Cw*02:02` → `HLA-C*02:02`
- **Long typings**: first two fields only (e.g. `HLA-DRB1*11:01:01:01` → `HLA-DRB1*11:01`)
- **Suffixes kept**: `HLA-A*01:04N`, `HLA-A*02:01G` (B-only; no A match)
